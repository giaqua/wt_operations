# Glossary

## Terms and Definitions

### Core Concepts

**WWTP (Wastewater Treatment Plant)**
A facility designed to treat wastewater from various sources before discharge or reuse.

**Technical Questionnaire (TQ)**
A comprehensive assessment document that captures project requirements, site conditions, and technical specifications for WWTP projects.

**Site Visit Request (SVR)**
A formal request for conducting on-site technical assessments and data collection.

**Site Visit (SV)**
The actual on-site assessment conducted by technical personnel to gather project-specific information.

**Water Sample**
A physical sample of wastewater collected for laboratory analysis to determine quality parameters.

**Lab Test Result (LTR)**
Laboratory analysis results of water samples, including compliance with regulatory standards.

**Technical Proposal**
A detailed technical solution document based on assessment findings and requirements.

**Customer Proposal**
A commercial document presenting technical solutions with pricing and commercial terms.

**Request for Proposal (RFP)**
A formal request from customers for technical and commercial proposals.

### Technical Terms

**Effluent**
Treated wastewater discharged from a treatment plant.

**Influent**
Raw wastewater entering a treatment plant.

**BOD5 (Biochemical Oxygen Demand)**
A measure of organic matter in wastewater, tested over 5 days.

**COD (Chemical Oxygen Demand)**
A measure of organic and inorganic matter in wastewater.

**TSS (Total Suspended Solids)**
Solid particles suspended in wastewater.

**TDS (Total Dissolved Solids)**
Dissolved substances in wastewater.

**pH**
A measure of acidity or alkalinity in water.

**Turbidity**
A measure of water clarity or cloudiness.

**E. coli**
Bacteria indicating fecal contamination in water.

**Free Chlorine**
Residual chlorine available for disinfection.

### Process Terms

**Auto-Fill**
Automated population of form fields based on related document data.

**Cross-Site Integration**
Synchronization of data between different ERPNext installations.

**External Site Settings**
Configuration for connecting to remote ERPNext sites.

**Sync Status**
Status of data synchronization between sites (Pending, Success, Failed, etc.).

**DocStatus**
Document status in ERPNext (Draft=0, Submitted=1, Cancelled=2).

**BOOT (Build-Own-Operate-Transfer)**
A project delivery model where the contractor builds, owns, and operates the facility before transferring it to the client.

### Roles and Permissions

**System Manager**
User with full system access and configuration privileges.

**Site Manager**
User responsible for managing site operations and technical assessments.

**Operations Technician**
User responsible for daily operations and data collection.

**Operations Manager**
User responsible for overseeing operations and managing complex projects.

**Account Manager**
User responsible for customer relationships and commercial aspects.

### Integration Terms

**API Key**
Authentication credential for accessing external site APIs.

**API Secret**
Secret authentication credential paired with API Key.

**Payload**
Data structure sent between sites during synchronization.

**Field Mapping**
Correspondence between local and remote site field names.

**Idempotency**
Property ensuring repeated operations produce the same result.

**Retry Logic**
Automatic retry mechanism for failed operations.

### Compliance Terms

**Discharge Standards**
Regulatory limits for treated wastewater quality parameters.

**Compliance Status**
Current status of regulatory compliance (Compliant, Non-Compliant, etc.).

**Environmental Impact Assessment**
Evaluation of environmental effects of WWTP operations.

**Discharge Permit**
Regulatory authorization for wastewater discharge.

**Environmental Monitoring**
Ongoing tracking of environmental parameters and compliance.

### Master Data

**Scope of Work**
Standardized work packages and responsibilities for WWTP projects.

**Wastewater Generator Type**
Classification of wastewater sources (Domestic, Industrial, etc.).

**Treatment Parameter**
Measurable water quality parameters used in treatment processes.

**Parameter Type**
Classification of parameters (Physical, Chemical, Biological, etc.).

**Tank Type**
Classification of storage and treatment tanks.

### Workflow Terms

**Lead**
Initial contact or opportunity for potential WWTP projects.

**Opportunity**
Qualified sales opportunity linked to a Lead.

**Follow-up Required**
Flag indicating additional actions needed after a process step.

**Priority**
Urgency level for tasks and requests (Low, Medium, High, Urgent).

**Status**
Current state of a document or process (Open, In Progress, Completed, etc.).

### Technical Specifications

**Design Capacity**
Maximum daily treatment capacity of a WWTP.

**Current Capacity**
Existing treatment capacity before upgrades.

**Peak Factor**
Ratio of peak flow to average flow.

**Operation Hours**
Daily hours of WWTP operation.

**Footprint**
Physical space required for WWTP installation.

**Equalization Tank**
Tank for balancing flow variations.

**Sludge Holding Tank**
Tank for storing treatment sludge.

**Product Tank**
Tank for storing treated water.
